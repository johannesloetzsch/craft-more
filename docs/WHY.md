# Why

## less slop → better vibe → more craft

Whatever your background, engaging thoroughly with modern agentic solutions pays off.

Without programming experience or command-line skills, modern tools let you dive quickly into the fascinating depths of computer science. To keep it from stopping at one-shot [slop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI_slop), the skills in this repository help you learn step by step and lift the quality to the level you want.

And even as an experienced nerd — nobody ever stops learning in computer science — there is still so much exciting left to explore.

[Vibe coding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vibe_coding) lets you try out new ideas quickly, whether you want just a [proof of concept (PoC)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proof_of_concept) or a larger [prototype](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_prototyping). What matters is defining the requirements for the result clearly and communicating the state of individual components honestly. [maturity-model](../skills/maturity-model/README.md) provides a scale with easily recognizable markers and symbols for exactly this.

Writing quality code is still a [craft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craft) — learning it takes a lot of practice. Used well, vibe coding can contribute to learning that craft. But don't just have the code generated; read it thoroughly, understand it, adapt it. If you want agency in the collaboration between agent and human, become aware of your own skills and bring them in.

Agents have also become a valuable power tool for experienced power users: virtual coworkers can take over the busywork while you do the requirements analysis; with the right system prompt, an agent can scrutinize the planning until a sharpened plan or design emerges from a relentless interview; in pair-programming between human and machine, both sides can contribute their strengths — but as far as possible under full user control; you can falsify the results together against the requirements and evaluate the quality of the outcome; and for IT security, joint protection-need and risk analyses and audits become possible that many projects would otherwise skip for lack of resources. What matters here: an agent alone does not yet replace an expert — it has to be operated, controlled, and verified properly.

## slop less, but vibe :) and craft more…

[**`You can create art and beauty on a computer`** and **`Computers can change your life for the better`**](https://www.ccc.de/en/hackerethik) :) So follow your vibes and vibe-code whenever a project seems worth it to you. After all, craft-more's core goal is to optimize a pleasant co-working experience with AI.

Collaboration with statistics-based helpers can lead to good results — but only when they receive more than the average as input. LLMs are trained on the average of the internet; their value doesn't come solely from the broad overview knowledge that statistics have baked into their weights. Above-average results from LLMs require above-average prompts.

Depending on the project context, different quality standards apply to the result. As a general rule: [**`Don't litter other people's data`** and **`Make public data available, protect private data`**](https://www.ccc.de/en/hackerethik).

## slop-less && vibe|bat && craft|more

The skills are orthogonal and composable in the spirit of the [unix philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unix_philosophy#Do_One_Thing_and_Do_It_Well): try a single one on its own, adopt the whole set, or combine a handful of small tools into something great.

With craft-more you are the `root` user: you control the prompt, the agent, the session, and the system instead of losing control.

When working with agents, fine-grained planning and a thorough review matter in particular. Craft-more gives you the tools to keep the overview.

For [prompt engineering / context engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prompt_engineering), it is important to be able to trace the chain of thought and to correct or support it as early as possible. Craft-more tries to collect skills that support introspection and grounding in facts.

## less slop, a better view, more craft — tools that show how we learn together

Skills are an easy way to define statistical rules for an LLM in a human- and machine-readable form.

Craft-more's greatest benefit comes from learning from past sessions, so that you don't have to point out the same mistakes to the agent again and again. Skills help with [inductive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inductive_reasoning) and [abductive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abductive_reasoning) reasoning to derive new rules for future collaboration.

Craft-more is a skill set that aims to build new skills through domain experts.

Sharing skills [freely](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_source) gives rise to [emergent systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergence).

Beyond that, craft-more's skills are meant to convey new knowledge to the user, so that we learn together.
